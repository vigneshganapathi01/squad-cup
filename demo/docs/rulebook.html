01Code of Conduct

2.1 General Behavior

All participants must maintain a respectful and professional attitude toward players, officials, and organizers. Violations may result in warnings, removal from an activity, or other appropriate action.

2.2 Sportsmanship

Offensive, abusive, threatening, or toxic language is strictly prohibited. Verbal abuse directed at participants, officials, or other community members may result in penalties.

2.3 Discrimination

Any form of discrimination based on race, gender, religion, nationality, or region is strictly forbidden. Harassment, cheating, or toxic conduct may lead to disciplinary action.

2.4 Punctuality

Participants must follow the timings and instructions communicated by the authorized organizers for the relevant activity.

2.5 Cooperation

Participants are expected to cooperate with authorized officials and complete any activity they have agreed to participate in, unless released by the organizers.

02Player & Team Regulations

3.1 Player Participation

Each player must participate using their own account and must not impersonate another player or misrepresent their identity.

3.2 Team Conduct

Players are responsible for their own conduct and must not encourage, assist, or conceal any rule violation by teammates.

03Match & Activity Rules

4.1 Official Instructions

Participants must follow the instructions issued by authorized Don Justice officials during organized activities.

04Match Ranking & Tiebreaker Rules

4.2 Standard Free Fire Tiebreaker Order

If two or more teams have the same total points at the end of the relevant set of matches, their final positions will be determined using the following tiebreaker criteria in the order listed below. The first criterion that separates the tied teams will determine their ranking.

a. Most Booyahs (1st-Place Finishes)

The team with the highest number of Booyah finishes (1st-place finishes) across the relevant set of matches will be ranked higher. Only the number of 1st-place finishes will be considered for this criterion.

b. Most Overall Kills

If the teams remain tied on the number of Booyahs, the team with the highest cumulative kill total across the relevant set of matches will be ranked higher. The total number of kills recorded by the team across those matches will be used for comparison.

c. Last Match Performance / Points

If the teams are still tied after comparing Booyahs and overall kills, their performance in the most recent (last) match will be considered. The team with the better placement and/or the higher points earned in that last match will be ranked higher.

4.3 Application of Tiebreakers

The criteria must be applied in the exact order stated above: Most Booyahs, then Most Overall Kills, and finally Last Match Performance / Points. A later criterion will only be used if the teams remain tied after the previous criterion has been compared.

The tiebreaker process applies only when teams have equal total points. It does not replace the normal points-based ranking system. Don Justice officials will verify the relevant match results and apply the criteria consistently to determine the final ranking.

05Device Restrictions

5.1 Authorized Devices & Software

Participants must use devices, game clients, and software permitted by Garena's applicable policies and any specific instructions issued for the activity. Modified clients, unauthorized third-party tools, scripts, hacks, or software that alters gameplay are prohibited.

5.2 Violations

Any use of unauthorized tools, modifications, or devices to gain an unfair advantage may result in removal from the activity and further disciplinary action.

06Streaming & Content Rules

6.1 Stream Sniping

Stream sniping and the use of live broadcast information to gain an unfair competitive advantage are strictly prohibited.

Verified cases may result in penalties or removal from the activity.

07Finality of Decisions & Rule Changes

All decisions made by authorized Don Justice officials regarding activities they administer are final.

Don Justice reserves the right to modify these general rules when required to maintain fairness, safety, or the integrity of its activities.

08Technical Issues

7.1 Individual Technical Issues

Participants are responsible for maintaining a suitable device, game installation, and internet connection. Individual technical issues may not automatically require an activity to be paused or restarted.

7.2 Major Technical Issues

If technical issues materially affect multiple participants or the integrity of an activity, officials may review the situation and decide on an appropriate action.

09Device, Recording & Fair Play Compliance Rules

8.1 Device Integrity

External peripherals, applications, or tools that are prohibited by Garena or by the specific activity rules must not be used.

Jailbroken devices, unauthorized emulators, modified game files, and tools that alter gameplay are not permitted where prohibited by applicable Garena policies or activity rules.

Don Justice officials may request reasonable verification where there is a legitimate concern about device or gameplay integrity.

Detection of unauthorized modifications, third-party tools, or game-altering software may result in immediate disciplinary action.

10Recording & POV Requirements

9.1 Recording

Participants may be required to keep gameplay recording enabled where this is communicated by the organizers for verification purposes.

9.2 Recording Submission

Authorized officials may request relevant gameplay footage or other evidence when reviewing a suspected violation.

Failure to provide requested evidence without a reasonable explanation may be considered during the review.

11Fair Play & Gameplay Monitoring

10.1 Suspicious Gameplay Investigations

If suspicious gameplay is reported, the participant concerned must cooperate with the authorized Don Justice officials and provide relevant information or evidence when reasonably requested.

Officials may review available gameplay recordings, screenshots, and other relevant evidence.

Failure to cooperate with a reasonable verification process may result in disciplinary action.

12Admin Enforcement Authority

11.1 Enforcement Actions

If hacks, restricted files, unauthorized configurations, or other serious violations are detected, Don Justice officials may take appropriate action.

Actions may include:

  • Warning
  • Removal from an activity
  • Suspension from future Don Justice activities

13Mandatory Compliance

12.1 Compliance Requests

All participants must comply with reasonable inspection, evidence, and verification requests made by authorized officials.

Refusal or failure to cooperate may result in removal from the activity.

14Device & Fair Play Compliance Policy

13.1 Device Type & Fair Play

Participants must use only the device type and setup permitted for the relevant activity and under applicable Garena policies.

Any unauthorized software, modifications, suspicious activity, or attempt to gain an unfair advantage may result in immediate disciplinary action.

15Final Authority Rules

14.1 Authority

All decisions concerning Don Justice activities, participant conduct, and fair-play reviews are subject to the authority of the designated Don Justice management team.

Don Justice reserves the right to make final decisions on matters or disputes relating to activities it organizes, based on the available evidence and applicable rules.

14.2 Management Rights

The management reserves the right to:

  • Modify or amend these rules when required for fairness, safety, or technical reasons.
  • Take appropriate disciplinary action, including warnings, removal, suspension, or bans from Don Justice activities.
  • Remove any participant whose conduct is dishonest, abusive, or harmful to the integrity of an activity.

All participants agree to abide by the decisions of the Don Justice management team, subject to applicable platform policies and law.
