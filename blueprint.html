01 / Easy example

The whole flow, step by step

Tournament: Chennai Squad Cup · 252 slots (set by the admin) · 6 Wild Card teams added by the admin. We follow one team: Team Thunder (Ravi, Arun, Karthik, Vijay).

1
Open the link and fill the form

Ravi opens the tournament link. The registration form opens straight away, with no sign-up. He types the team name "Team Thunder" and the 4 players' name, mobile, email and Free Fire UID. Ravi is Player 1, so he is the captain and contact.

2
The system checks and gives a Team ID

It checks that every field is filled, mobiles have 10 digits, emails are valid, and no UID is already in another team. After submitting, Ravi sees Team ID: FF-0427 and the status Pending. He saves it and shares it with his team.

3
Admin approves

The admin sees all registrations in one table. Problems are flagged automatically (for example, the same UID used twice). The admin approves Team Thunder, and the status changes to Approved ✅.

4
Groups are made automatically

All 252 slots are filled, and every group has 12 teams, so 252 ÷ 12 = 21 groups (A to U). Team Thunder lands in Group C, Slot 7. The admin can swap teams if needed, then locks and publishes the groups.

5
Team opens its dashboard

Anyone in the team opens "My Team" and enters FF-0427 + Ravi's mobile. They see: Group C · Slot 7 · Match 1 today at 7:00 PM.

6
Check-in and room details

Check-in opens at 6:30 PM and Ravi taps Check in. At 6:45 PM the room creator posts Room ID 1234567 · Password ff22. Only checked-in Group C teams can see them. The players join the custom room in Free Fire and play.

7
Results and points

The room creator enters each team's position and kills and uploads screenshots. Team Thunder finished 2nd (9 points) + 7 kills (7 points) = 16 points. The admin checks the screenshots and approves, and the leaderboard updates live. Each group plays 6 matches, and the points add up.

8
Qualify, stage by stage

The admin approves who goes through, and the next stage's groups are made automatically.

StageTeamsGroupsWho goes throughNext
Main Stage 125221Top 8 of each group168
Main Stage 216814Top 684
Main Stage 3847Top 642
9
Admin picks 6 Wild Card teams

The admin chooses 6 special teams (for example creators, YouTubers or invited teams) and types their 4 players into the Wild Card screen. They don't play Stages 1–3.

10
Wild Cards enter at Stage 4

42 qualified teams + 6 Wild Cards = 48 teams in 4 groups. The admin chooses which group and slot each Wild Card goes into. Wild Card teams keep a "WC" tag. Stage 4: 48 → 24, then Stage 5: 24 → 12.

11
Grand Final, over 2 days

The 12 teams play 6 matches on Day 1 and 6 on Day 2. Points carry over, and the highest total after 12 matches is the 🏆 Champion. If two teams are tied, the winner is decided by most Booyahs, then position points, then kills.

Team dashboard

Mobile-first, because players open it on their phone just before a match.

What it shows, top to bottom

  • Next step card (changes automatically): "Waiting for approval" → "Approved: Group C, Slot 7" → "Check in now (closes 6:45 PM)" → "Room ID 1234567 · Pass ff22 · starts 7:00 PM" with copy buttons
  • Team: name, Team ID, 4 players with UIDs (mobiles partly hidden: 98xxxxxx21)
  • Upcoming matches with countdown
  • Results: position, kills, points, rank in group
  • Status: Qualified / Eliminated after each stage

Rules

  • Opens only with the right Team ID + captain mobile; it stays logged in on that phone for 30 days
  • Room details show only after check-in, and only from 15 minutes before the start
  • Times in IST
  • Leaderboard updates live
  • Light pages for slow mobile data
07

Admin dashboard

Overview

  • Counts: registered, pending, approved, matches today, results waiting, complaints
  • Today's match timeline
  • Alerts: room details missing 10 minutes before the start, results late, a group over capacity

Setup

  • Tournament name, dates, rules, prize text
  • Admin sets only the number of slots (e.g. 252)
  • Groups of 12, Top 8 / Top 6, the Wild Card stage and number of stages are calculated by the fixed logic (section 9)
  • Live preview of all stages and the bracket updates as the number changes
  • After registration closes, the plan uses the real approved count

Registrations

  • Table of teams with their 4 players, search and filter
  • Auto flags: duplicate UID or mobile, banned player
  • Approve / reject with a reason (bulk approve too)
  • Import Google Form CSV · Export to Excel

Groups

  • Generate: in order / random / seeded
  • Move or swap teams; group size can't go over the maximum
  • Draft → Lock → Publish (unlocking needs a reason)

Slots

  • Admin sets total slots (default 252; can be any number, e.g. 180, 300, 500)
  • Each registered team takes one slot: Slot 1, Slot 2 … Slot 252
  • Form shows slots left, and closes when they're full; later teams go on a waitlist
  • If a team is rejected or withdraws, its slot goes to the first team on the waitlist
  • Admin can raise or lower the limit before groups are generated

Wild Card entry (admin only)

  • Screen: "Add Wild Card" with team name + 4 players (name, mobile, email, UID), same checks as the form
  • 6 Wild Cards; they get a Team ID and a "WC" tag
  • When the Wild Card stage is generated (Stage 4 for 252 slots), the admin chooses the group and slot for each Wild Card, or clicks "Auto place" to spread them evenly
  • Groups still never go over 12
  • Every add, move or remove is written in the log

Schedule

  • Matches created automatically for each group
  • Set date, time and map, and assign a room creator

Results & qualifying

  • Results next to their screenshots → edit (with a reason) → approve → publish
  • Stage leaderboard with tie-breakers
  • System suggests the Top N, admin confirms → next stage generated from the real count

Rules, points and stages (full plan)

The logic below is fixed. The only number the admin types is the number of slots (e.g. 252). The admin also adds the 6 Wild Card teams by hand. Everything else is calculated.

A. The fixed logic

#RuleFixed value
1Teams per group (one Free Fire custom room)12, always
2Number of groupsTeams ÷ 12, rounded up
3If teams don't divide by 12 exactlySpread evenly: groups differ by at most 1 team (e.g. 11 and 12). Never more than 12.
4Main Stage 1 qualifiersTop 8 of each group
5Every later stageTop 6 of each group
6Wild Card teamsDefault 6 (admin can set 0–12), added by the admin (no qualifying matches)
7Where the Wild Cards joinThe first stage where qualified teams + 6 Wild Cards fit in 5 groups or fewer (≤ 60 teams)
8Stages keep running until12 teams are left
9Grand FinalAlways 12 teams, 1 group, 12 matches over 2 days
10Matches per group in every stage6 matches (Grand Final: 6 + 6)

The system uses the real number of approved teams. If the admin sets 252 slots but only 230 are approved, it plans for 230. Minimum: 24 slots.

180S1 180→15g→120 · S2 120→10g→60 · S3 60→5g→30 · S4 30+6=36→3g→18 · S5 18→2g→1212
300S1 300→25g→200 · S2 200→17g→102 · S3 102→9g→54 · S4 54+6=60→5g→30 · S5 30→3g→18 · S6 18→2g→1212

The number of stages changes by itself: 252 teams need 5 stages before the final, 500 teams need 6. The bold stage is where the 6 Wild Cards join.

C. Points for every match

PositionPointsPositionPoints
1st (Booyah)127th4
2nd98th3
3rd89th2
4th710th1
5th611th0
6th512th0
  • Each kill = 1 point
  • Match points = position points + kill points
  • Stage points = total of the 6 matches
  • Example: 2nd place + 7 kills = 9 + 7 = 16

D. If two teams have equal points

  1. More Booyahs (1st places) in the stage
  2. More total position points
  3. More total kills
  4. Better position in the last match

E. Grand Final

  • 12 teams, Day 1: 6 matches, Day 2: 6 matches
  • Day 1 points carry over to Day 2
  • Highest total after 12 matches = Champion; then 2nd and 3rd

F. Team and registration rules

  • 4 players per team, all with a Free Fire UID
  • A player (UID or mobile) can be in only one team
  • Players must be the same as registered; a different UID in the room = 0 points for that match
  • Team name: no bad words, no copying other teams' names
  • Players 18+ or with a parent's permission

G. Match day rules

  • Check in on the dashboard before the deadline (30 min before the match)
  • Join the room using your slot number; joining the wrong slot = warning, then 0 points
  • Room ID and password are only for your team; sharing them = disqualified
  • Room closes at start time; late teams get 0 points for that match
  • No restart for disconnects or lag
  • Map order is set by the admin for each stage (e.g. Bermuda, Purgatory, Kalahari)

H. Fair play

  • No hacks, mods, panels or emulators unless the admin allows them: disqualification + ban
  • No teaming with other teams: disqualification
  • No bug or glitch abuse
  • Room creator and admin can spectate and report

I. Results and complaints

  • Results come from the room creator's screenshots
  • Complaints within 30 minutes of the result, with a screenshot
  • The admin's decision is final
  • 2 no-shows in one stage = removed from the tournament

Security and fair play

Security

  • Only staff have passwords; teams use Team ID + captain mobile
  • Limit wrong attempts on the team dashboard (5 tries, then wait 15 minutes)
  • Hide mobiles and emails from the public and other teams
  • Screenshots stored privately, images only
  • Every admin change logged
  • Collect no Aadhaar or ID numbers

Fair play

  • One UID in one team only
  • Ban list by UID and mobile
  • Room details released late and only to checked-in teams
  • Room creator reports unknown players in the room
  • Every result needs screenshots
Weak point: anyone who knows the Team ID and captain mobile can open the dashboard. That's fine for a prototype; add an OTP later.
14

Legal and brand checks

Check before launch (not legal advice):
  • Entry fees and cash prizes: India's online gaming law of 2025 bans online money games; e-sports are treated separately. Keep the prototype free to enter and ask a lawyer before taking fees.
  • Game: confirm the Free Fire version your players use (e.g. Free Fire MAX) is officially available in India.
  • Brand: Free Fire belongs to Garena. Use your own logo and say "Community tournament, not affiliated with Garena".
  • Minors: add an 18+ or parent's-permission checkbox to the form.
  • Privacy: you collect mobiles and emails, so add a short privacy note (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).
